Features

Built for watch collectors

From a first automatic to a case full of grails — track what you own, what you wear, and what you're hunting.

Full Spec Sheets

Brand, reference, movement, case and lug size, crystal, water resistance, and a photo gallery for every watch.

Portfolio Value

Track purchase price against current estimates, watch your collection's value over time, and see your top holdings.

Service & Accuracy

Log services and set reminders before the next one is due. Record timing checks and see accuracy drift as a trend.

Wear Tracking

One tap logs the day's watch. Streaks, a wear heat strip, cost-per-wear, and which pieces are overdue for rotation.

Grail Wishlist & Alerts

Keep a wishlist of the watches you're hunting and get a daily digest of new eBay listings that match — by push and email.

Yours, Offline First

Your collection lives on your device and works signed-out. Optional cloud sync, public profiles, and groups are opt-in.
