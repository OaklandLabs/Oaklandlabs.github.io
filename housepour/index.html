Your home bar, organized. Track the bottles you own and see which cocktails you can make tonight, and which bottle to buy next.

Features

Built for the home bartender

From a few bottles on the counter to a full back bar: know what you have, what you can make, and what's worth adding.

Your Bar, on a Shelf

Every bottle you own, with a drag-to-set fill level. Housepour flags what's nearly empty or out, and one tap puts a refill on your shopping list.

What Can I Make?

Hundreds of classic and modern cocktails, sorted by what your bar can make right now and which ones are a single bottle away.

Buy This Next

See which single bottle would unlock the most new drinks, and keep a shopping list for your next run.

Scan a Label

Photograph a bottle and Housepour reads the label on your device to fill in the brand and type. The photo never leaves your phone.

Plan a Party

Pick the drinks for the night, scale any recipe to a crowd, and get one combined shopping list.

Private by Design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your bar lives on your device, and backups are a file you keep.
