Privacy Policy

Provenant by Oakland Labs LLC — Last Updated: November 2026

1. The short version

Your collection is yours. We never sell your collection, and we never give advertisers your email address, your items, or your photos. Provenant needs a free account (email address and username). On the free tier your collection stays on your device, except items you choose to make public; with Premium, it syncs to our cloud so it’s on all your devices.

The free tier shows banner ads from Google AdMob; Premium removes them. Ads are only personalized if you allow tracking (iPhone) or consent to it (EEA, UK, and Switzerland); see section 5. We also use crash reporting and product analytics, tied to your account, to fix bugs and improve the app.

3. Service providers

We use a small number of providers, each limited to the purpose listed:

  • Supabase: accounts, cloud database, and photo storage.
  • RevenueCat: subscription status and purchase history (no card data).
  • Google AdMob: ads in the free tier (section 5).
  • Sentry: crash and error reporting.
  • PostHog: product analytics.
  • Resend: sending account, subscription, and wishlist alert emails.
  • Expo: delivering push notifications.
  • eBay: to estimate an item’s value or find listings for your wishlist, our servers send a description of the item (not your name or email) to eBay’s public API. We participate in the eBay Partner Network, so listing links carry an affiliate tag and we may earn a commission on a purchase, at no extra cost to you.
  • PSA and Wikidata: looking up a certification number or a player you enter.

4. Public profiles, community, and notifications

Public profiles, public items, and groups are opt-in, and you choose which items appear publicly. Group leaderboards mostly show aggregate statistics rather than item details. When you like something, the owner can see that you liked it and is notified, and your likes help personalize your feed. You can block other collectors.

If you allow notifications, we send pushes for activity like new followers, likes, group joins, and wishlist matches, and occasionally a reminder if you haven’t opened the app in a while. Wishlist alert emails are a daily digest you turn on; each has a one-click unsubscribe link. You can turn any of these off in the app’s settings. We don’t send marketing email.

5. Advertising and tracking

The free tier shows banner ads served by Google AdMob; Premium removes them. To show ads, Google’s software receives device information (such as model, operating system, and IP address, from which it may estimate your general location), ad interactions, and, only if you allow it, your device’s advertising identifier. We never give Google or any advertiser your email address, collection, or photos.

  • iPhone: we ask through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, the advertising identifier isn’t shared and ads aren’t personalized. Change it any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can delete your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your device’s Settings → Google → Ads.
  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland: we show Google’s consent form, and ads are only personalized if you consent.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

6. Your rights

We do not sell personal information for money. When you allow personalized ads, the information Google’s ads software receives may count as “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising under some U.S. state laws, including California’s. You can opt out at any time by declining tracking or ads personalization as described in section 5, or by upgrading to Premium. You also have the right to know what we collect and to have it deleted, and we won’t treat you differently for using these rights.

EU and UK residents have rights under GDPR to access, correct, restrict the processing of, object to, port, and erase their personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. We rely on your consent for personalized ads and tracking, and on our legitimate interest in running and improving the service for crash reporting and analytics. To exercise any of these rights, email support@oaklandlabs.app; we respond within 30 days.

7. Deleting your data

In the app you can delete your cloud data while keeping your account, or delete your account and everything in it; see Delete Account or Data. Crash reports and analytics events are kept by Sentry and PostHog for a limited period and then deleted.

8. Retention and security

We keep account data while your account is active. When you delete your account, cloud data is permanently deleted within 30 days. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.

9. Children

Provenant is not directed at children. You must be at least 13 years old to create an account. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes and contact

If we make a material change to this policy, we’ll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app. Questions? Email support@oaklandlabs.app or call (509) 381-3920.
