Binderly - Card Collection

Track your Pokémon TCG collection. Scan cards to add them, know what your collection is worth, build decks that pass legality, and never miss a deal.

Coming soon to the App Store

iOS and Android planned at launch. Want to hear when it ships? Email support@oaklandlabs.app.

Features

Built for card collectors

From a starter binder to a full set run — track what you own, what it's worth, and what's worth chasing.

Scan to Add

Point your camera at a card and Binderly identifies it on-device — no photo ever leaves your phone.

Collection Value

Market price baselines for every card, with live eBay comps for the pieces worth watching closely.

Deck Builder

Build decks from your own collection, check format legality, and see what you're missing to complete one.

Grading ROI Advisor

Look up a card's PSA population and pricing to see whether submitting it for grading is worth the cost.

Wishlist & Deal Alerts

Keep a wishlist of the cards you're hunting and get a digest of new eBay listings that match.

Yours, Offline First

Your collection lives on your device and works signed-out. Optional cloud sync, profiles, and groups are opt-in.
