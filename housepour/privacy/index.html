Privacy Policy

Housepour by Oakland Labs LLC — Last Updated: September 2026

1. The short version

Housepour keeps your bar on your device. There is no account, and we run no servers that receive your data. Your bottles, recipes, notes, shopping list, drink log, and photos are stored only on your phone. We never sell, rent, or trade personal data, and the app has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. The only information that leaves your device is what’s needed to process an optional Housepour Pro purchase, described below.

2. What stays on your device

Data Why Where it lives
Your bars and bottles (names, brands, sizes, fill levels, notes, favorites) The core function of the app On your device only
Recipes you create, favorites, recipe notes, and ratings Your personal recipe book On your device only
Shopping list and drink log Planning purchases and remembering what you’ve made On your device only
Bottle photos Photos you choose to attach to a bottle On your device only
Label scans Reading a bottle label to fill in its brand and type Processed on your device; never uploaded
Settings, including your answer to the drinking-age question Remembering your preferences On your device only

3. Housepour Pro purchases

Housepour Pro is an optional one-time purchase made through the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or collect your payment details. To confirm and restore your purchase, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID, your purchase history for Housepour, and the technical information any app request carries (such as device type, operating system, app version, and IP address). This information is used only to confirm that you own Housepour Pro. It is not linked to your name or email, and it is not used for advertising or tracking. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Apple and Google process purchases under their own privacy policies.

4. Camera, photos, and label scanning

Housepour asks for camera or photo-library access only when you take or choose a picture of a bottle. Label scanning uses Google’s ML Kit text recognition, which runs entirely on your device: the photo and the text read from it are not sent to us or to Google. Google’s ML Kit software may send Google limited, anonymous diagnostic and performance information (such as device type and how long recognition took) to maintain the feature; it does not include your photos or the text on them. See Google’s ML Kit terms. You can deny camera or photo access and still use the rest of the app.

5. Backups

You can export a backup of your bar as a file through your device’s share sheet. That file goes wherever you choose to send or save it (for example Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or email) and is handled under that service’s policies. We never receive a copy. Backups do not include bottle photos.

6. Links and email

Links in the app to this site open in your browser or an in-app browser. This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and collects no personal data. If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply.

7. Deleting your data

Because your data lives on your device, deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no account to close. To have RevenueCat’s purchase record for your anonymous app user ID deleted, email support@oaklandlabs.app; you would then need to restore your purchase through the App Store or Google Play to use Housepour Pro again.

8. Your rights

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request its deletion, and to opt out of its sale; we do not sell personal information to anyone. EU and UK residents have rights under GDPR to access, correct, restrict the processing of, port, and erase their personal data. Since almost everything stays on your device, most of these rights are exercised directly in the app. For anything else, email support@oaklandlabs.app; we respond within 30 days.

9. Security

Data on your device is protected by your device’s own security. Purchase information sent to RevenueCat, Apple, and Google is encrypted in transit (TLS).

10. Age

Housepour is about alcoholic drinks and is intended only for adults of legal drinking age where they live. It is not directed at children or anyone under the legal drinking age, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe someone under age has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes and contact

If we make a material change to this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, note it in the app. Questions? Email support@oaklandlabs.app or call (509) 381-3920.
