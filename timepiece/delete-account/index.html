Permanent

Delete Your Account

Deleting your account permanently removes your Timepiece Tracker account and every piece of data we hold in the cloud for it. This cannot be undone.

How to delete your account in the app:

  1. Open the Timepiece Tracker app on your device.
  2. Tap the Settings tab (gear icon).
  3. Scroll to the Account section and tap Manage account.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

What is deleted:

Data Outcome Retention
Your account & email address Permanently deleted None
Synced watches & specs Permanently deleted Purged from backups within 30 days
Watch photos in the cloud Permanently deleted Purged from backups within 30 days
Wishlist items & alert settings Permanently deleted None
Profile, display name, username, avatar Permanently deleted None
Group memberships Removed. Groups you created are deleted unless another owner remains None
Push notification token Permanently deleted — alerts stop immediately None
Local data on your device Kept on your device Until you uninstall the app
Subscription / billing history Managed by Apple / Google — not deleted by us Per Apple / Google policy

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it separately in your device's subscription settings (iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions; Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions).

To also remove the copy stored on your device, uninstall the app after deleting your account.

Can't access the app? Email support@oaklandlabs.app from your account's email address and we will delete your account manually within 30 days.
Keeps Account

Delete Your Cloud Data (Without Deleting Your Account)

You can erase your cloud-stored collection while keeping your account, so you can keep signing in, start fresh, or simply stop syncing.

How to delete your cloud data in the app:

  1. Open the Timepiece Tracker app on your device.
  2. Tap the Settings tab (gear icon).
  3. In the Account section, tap Manage account.
  4. Tap Delete Cloud Data.
  5. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

What is deleted vs. kept:

Data Outcome Retention
Synced watches & specs (cloud) Permanently deleted from cloud Purged from backups within 30 days
Wishlist items (cloud) Permanently deleted from cloud None
Your account & email address Kept — account remains active Until you delete your account
Profile & group memberships Kept Until you delete your account or leave the group
Local data on your device Kept on your device Until you uninstall the app
Note: with cloud sync still enabled, the collection on your device will upload again on the next sync. Turn sync off first under Settings → Account → Cloud sync if you want the cloud to stay empty.
Emails Only

Just Stop the Emails

To stop wishlist digest emails without touching your data, turn off Email digest under Settings → Notifications, or use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any digest. Both take effect immediately.
